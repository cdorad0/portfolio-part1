Data Description

  1. Identify your data source. > My data source is derived from DataONE [https://search.dataone.org/view/doi%3A10.25494%2FP69885] and is labelled “Reef Check kelp forest long-term MPA monitoring: invertebrate data”

  2. Describe your data, including variables and data types. > The data I selected is from Reef Check California (RCCA), a non-profit organization that monitors kelp forests in and outside of California’s Marine Protected Areas in efforts to improve costal management, research, and conservation. The data includes invertebrate surveys that consist of visual surveys performed by scuba divers. Key species of invertebrates were monitored at sites divided into two “zones” by depth from inshore to offshore. Invertebrates are counted on 6 transects per site and were counted if they were larger than 2.5 centimeters. Variables include: Site, Day, Month, Year, SurveyDate, Transect, Classcode, Amount, Distance, Latitude, Longitude, Depth_ft, Temp10m, Visibility. Data types consists primarly of characters and doubles.

  3. Identify the research questions you want to answer. > “Are there seasonal patterns in abalone species counts, and have they shifted over time?” > “Is there evidence of recovery or decline in abalone populations in surveyed areas from 2006 to 2019?”
